# Economics and finance

I worked on data calibration, literature review and econometric analysis in Stata for a study of carbon emissions in Macau. We used STIRPAT, EKC and LMDI models to examine changes in emissions. [Paper and authors](../publications.md)

My earlier finance work explored forecasting the term structure of interest rates with dynamic stochastic-volatility models. A question I'd like to study next is how much of a rate movement around a policy announcement reflects a policy surprise, and how much reflects new information about the economy.
