# Economic and financial research

## Carbon emissions in Macau

I contributed to **The Determinants of Carbon Emissions in Macau: Based on the Analysis of STIRPAT, EKC, and LMDI Models**, published in 2023. My work included data calibration, literature review, and econometric analysis using Stata.

[Read the publication](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-99-3618-2_15)

The project made data comparability and interpretation concrete concerns for me. A fitted relationship can be informative without identifying the effect of a policy intervention. I want subsequent work to state that distinction clearly.

## Interest-rate term structure

My earlier finance research explored forecasting the term structure of interest rates using dynamic stochastic-volatility models. This is a historical research project; a reproducible public package would need the original manuscript, data provenance, transformations, source code, baseline definitions, and result tables.

## Questions for further study

I am interested in what interest-rate movements around policy announcements reflect: a policy surprise, changed information about the economy, or other contemporaneous news. Forecasting performance alone does not identify those mechanisms.

A future empirical study should define the event, observation window, competing explanations, identification assumptions, and robustness checks before drawing a causal conclusion. This is a proposed research direction rather than a completed causal study.
