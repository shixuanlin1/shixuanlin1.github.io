# Greenworks | Which brands belong in the comparison?

In a fall 2024 NYU Real World client project for Greenworks, I compared nine outdoor power equipment brands. Two of them had exactly the same price band in our worksheet. That raised a better question for positioning. Would the same buyer actually put those products on the same shortlist?

| Selected brand | Price range in the 2024 project worksheet |
| --- | ---: |
| Greenworks | $100–400 |
| Kress | $100–400 |
| EGO | $250–600 |

Greenworks and Kress overlap throughout the recorded range. EGO overlaps only in part. The worksheet groups equipment by brand, so these ranges are a starting point for choosing what to investigate. They cannot tell me whether two comparable mowers cost the same.

For a commercial landscaping crew, I would narrow the comparison to one equipment class and duty cycle, then check what is included in the price. A bare tool and a kit with batteries can look like different price positions even when the difference is the bundle. For a dealer, that changes the conversation from a blanket discount to what the crew needs for a full shift.

The class workbook also contains two sets of customer ratings with different values for several brands. I left rankings out of this comparison. The price ranges come from the project's separate price worksheet and describe the 2024 exercise.
